# Privacy Policy — Hermes (personal use)

This is a personal, self-hosted AI agent used solely by its operator. It is not
offered as a service to other users.

**What it accesses.** Where authorized via Google OAuth, this app reads and may
send email, and reads/writes calendar, Drive, Sheets, and Docs data — only as
instructed by the operator.

**Where data lives.** OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's own
machines. Accessed content is processed locally and stored in local
logs/memory only as needed for the operator's workflows.

**Third-party processing.** Prompts and content may be processed by the
large-language-model providers the operator has configured, under their
respective privacy policies. No Google user data is sold or shared for
advertising or any other purpose.

**Deletion.** Authorization can be revoked at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions; local data can be deleted on request
by the operator.
